Chicago Serious Crash Statistical Model

R Neg. Binomial GLM

Compared five statistical models to predict daily serious crash counts in Chicago using day-of-week, month, road surface, and weather predictors. A negative binomial regression (log link) achieved the best fit, revealing weekends and summer months as the strongest drivers of serious crashes.

Best Model: NB (AIC: 8551.7)
RMSE: 2.55 crashes/day
